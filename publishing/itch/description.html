A little sea. Four seasons. A quiet moment.

Welcome to FixelFishing, a small pixel-art fishing scene made for slowing down. Sail your little yacht, let the angler cast, and watch a full day pass in a minute.

  • Four smoothly changing seasons, drifting birds and a living shoreline.
  • Layered rain and snow, accumulating snow, wind and optional thunderstorms.
  • A retractable boat canopy, winter clothes, warm cabin windows and a sweeping night searchlight that gently attracts fish.
  • Six original fish species, automatic lure fishing and a collapsible collection board with counts, rarity points and achievements.
  • A glowing lure, beach campfire and optional thunder audio. Sound and lightning start switched off.

How to play

  • Drag the boat or click/tap the water: sail left or right. Fishing is automatic, with a 50% chance of landing each hooked fish.
  • Collection button: open the fish cards; click/tap a card for details. Escape closes the board.
  • Season/weather labels: preview conditions; use settings to return to automatic.
  • Drag the sky: pan. Mouse wheel: zoom. H or settings: adjust the scene and opt into thunder audio or lightning.

Requires a WebGL-capable browser with hardware acceleration. Responsive controls support pointer and touch input; performance varies by device. Collection progress is stored in this browser for this site, not in a cloud account. Clearing site data can erase it; restricted embedded storage falls back to session-only progress.

一小片海，一段慢下来的时光

拖动游艇或点击海面，钓鱼人会自动抛投、等待与鱼拉锯。看四季昼夜、层次丰富的雨雪与自然风场，收集六种原创鱼，点亮图鉴和成就。雨雪时展开船篷，冬季换上冬装；夜间舱窗、探照灯、鱼饵和沙滩篝火渐次亮起，鱼群靠近光束边缘。

图鉴记录在当前网站的浏览器本地，不跨设备同步。雷声与闪电默认关闭，可在设置中主动开启。暂停只冻结海浪、自由鱼和钓鱼，昼夜与天气仍继续。
