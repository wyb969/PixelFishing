A little sea. A quiet moment.

Welcome to PixelFish, a small pixel-art fishing scene made for slowing down. Find a spot on the turquoise water, let your angler cast, and watch a whole day drift past in one minute.

Stay for the sunset

  • Gentle waves, drifting clouds, seabirds and a palm-lined shore.
  • Fish that chase your lure, bite, dart away and put up a fight.
  • Automatic fishing with a 50% chance of landing each hooked fish.
  • A glowing lure and a warm beach campfire after dark.

How to play

  • Drag the boat or click the water: sail left or right.
  • Fishing: automatic — settle in and watch.
  • Drag the sky: pan the view. Mouse wheel: zoom.
  • H: show settings for waves, speed and pixel size.

Play directly in your desktop browser with WebGL and hardware acceleration enabled. This is an experimental relaxing scene, with a fixed side-view composition.

一小片海，一段慢下来的时光

拖动小船，选一处喜欢的海面。钓鱼人会自动抛投、等待、与鱼拉锯；水下的鱼会追饵、挣扎，也可能逃走。一分钟看完昼夜，入夜后还有发光鱼饵与沙滩篝火。
